## Core Capabilities

### 🔍 Handwriting Intelligence
Reads and understands handwritten student responses — including diagrams, equations, and margin notes — with **98% accuracy**. No need to digitize or re-type anything.

### 📝 Smart Paper Builder
Create question papers with full **LaTeX/math support**, image attachments, and OR-question pairing. Upload an existing paper PDF and let AI extract questions automatically via OCR.

### 🤖 AI Rubric Engine
Provide model answers → AI generates detailed, criterion-based rubrics with customizable weights. Supports multiple valid answer approaches per question.

### ⚡ QuickPass™ Paper Analysis
Catch problems before the exam happens:
- **Ambiguity Detection** — Flags unclear or confusing questions
- **Difficulty-Marks Alignment** — Are 2-mark questions actually 2-mark-hard?
- **Duplicate Detection** — Catches overlapping questions
- **OR Pair Balance** — Ensures paired questions are equally weighted
- **Paper Health Score** — Single 0–100 metric for paper quality

### 📊 Syllabus Coverage Tracking
Link your course syllabus → see exactly which topics your paper covers, which are missing, and how coverage is distributed across units. Visual breakdown per unit with uncovered topic warnings.

### 🚀 Parallel Evaluation at Scale
Upload up to **500 scanned answer sheets** at once. AI processes them in parallel — each sheet scored, annotated, and ready for review in **~15 seconds**.

### 📄 Professional Reports
- Interactive web reports with criterion-level breakdowns
- Downloadable **branded PDF reports** (cover page, score tables, student answer images, feedback cards)
- One-click student account creation for result sharing

### 💬 Student Feedback Loop
Students log in, view their detailed evaluation report, and submit feedback — closing the loop between assessment and learning.

## How It Works

```
1. Create Paper        → Build or upload question paper + model answers
2. Generate Rubrics    → AI creates criterion-based scoring rubrics
3. QuickPass Check     → AI validates paper quality before the exam
4. Collect Submissions → Bulk upload scanned answer sheet PDFs
5. AI Evaluation       → Handwriting recognition + rubric-based scoring
6. Review & Publish    → Faculty reviews AI scores, approves with one click
7. Share Results       → Students get detailed reports + feedback channel
```
